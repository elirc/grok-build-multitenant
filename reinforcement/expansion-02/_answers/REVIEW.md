# Sealed capstone and review notes

Reject a patch that trusts organizationId from the payload over verified context, accepts every unknown action for admins, or retries conflicts by fetching the newest version automatically. Each changes a separate contract and needs a concrete counterexample.

Prefer a small existing-boundary regression over a new authorization abstraction unless the current design prevents consistent enforcement. Require the learner to point to the real route, service, and repository predicate. Their capstone should demonstrate one authorized success and independent scope, capability, validation, and stale-write failures without conflating them.

## Assess a learner's explanation

Ask for the actual input and observable result, then change one assumption. A strong explanation adapts the test or identifies a missing product decision. A weak explanation repeats the reference implementation without explaining why its boundary belongs there.

Do not award runtime evidence for a written plan. Keep “source supports this,” “model reproduced this,” and “application reproduced this” as separate statements. If an existing implementation already handles a scenario, the useful contribution may be a test and a clearer contract rather than another fix.
